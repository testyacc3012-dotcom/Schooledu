Our method

Three habits, held all the way through

Every course at Meridian, from times tables to related rates, is taught the same underlying way.

FIG. 1

Concrete before abstract

Students build or measure something before they name it. A formula only gets introduced once the pattern behind it has already been noticed by hand.

FIG. 2

Paced by mastery, not by month

Nobody moves to the next unit on a calendar. They move when a short, unglamorous check shows the last one actually stuck.

FIG. 3

Every unit ends in a real question

Compound interest ends with a real loan offer. Geometry ends with a room that needs tiling. The last problem is never made up.

Course tracks

Four tracks, one continuous line

Students place into a track after a short diagnostic, then move at their own pace inside it.

TRACK 01

Foundations

Grades 3–5

Number sense, fractions, and early measurement, built through games, manipulatives, and a lot of estimating before calculating.

TRACK 02

Algebra Studio

Grades 6–8

Variables as a language for patterns. Students write their own word problems before they ever solve someone else's.

TRACK 03

Geometry Lab

Grades 8–10

Proof as argument, not ritual. Compass, straightedge, and increasingly, code, used to test conjectures before proving them.

TRACK 04

Calculus Seminar

Grades 11–12

Rates of change grounded in physics and economics problems students choose themselves, seminar-style, ten students to a table.
